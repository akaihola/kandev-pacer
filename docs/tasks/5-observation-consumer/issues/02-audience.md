# Who is the consumer's audience?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Who acts on what the consumer produces: Antti reading a review, an agent (Tyko or a
Kandev task) that takes action, or the pacer as a machine reader? The answer fixes the
form of everything downstream, so it is decided before home, output and cadence.

## Proposed answer (needs Antti)

Antti, through an agent-drafted review. Reasons: the pacer as reader is closed
(ticket five); a fully autonomous agent that changes workflows or knobs has nothing
to check itself against yet, since no review has ever been read; and the vault's own
history says unread digests get dropped. So the first consumer produces a short
review for Antti, and a later effort can hand parts of it to an agent once the
review has proven useful. Question to settle live: does Antti want to read a
review at all, or only be interrupted when a record shows a clear problem (an errored
call storm, a step that took hours, a repeated command)?
