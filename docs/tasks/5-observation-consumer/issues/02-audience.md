# Who is the consumer's audience?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Who acts on what the consumer produces: Antti reading a review, an agent (Tyko or a
Kandev task) that takes action, or the pacer as a machine reader? The answer fixes the
form of everything downstream, so it is decided before home, output and cadence.

## Answer

Decided by Antti, 2026-09-13.

A periodic review. Problems found in the records are brought to Antti's attention so
that Antti and the agent collaborate on an improvement to the agentic system that
mitigates the problem. The consumer is not a silent machine reader and not an
autonomous fixer: it observes, proposes, and hands the decision to Antti.
